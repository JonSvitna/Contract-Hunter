# Local Source Strategy

## Why local-first

The objective is realistic solo wins. Local and county procurements are more likely to contain opportunities with practical scope, faster relationship development, and less overhead than large federal programs.

## Priority bands

1. County government purchasing pages.
2. Municipal procurement and bids pages.
3. School district procurement pages.
4. Public library and utility authority opportunities.
5. eMMA as a secondary source.

## Match profile

- Contract values around $2k-$25k preferred, up to $50k acceptable.
- Scope suited for one consultant.
- Cybersecurity policy, assessments, vulnerability, and compliance assistance.

## Exclusion/down-rank profile

- Federal contracts.
- Massive statewide contracts.
- Team-heavy managed services and 24/7 support models.
- Unrealistic deadlines.
