# Roadmap

## Phase 1 (current MVP)

- Local source discovery and scoring.
- Dashboard and status management.
- Config-driven profile and keyword targeting.

## Phase 2

- Email digest summary of newly discovered opportunities.
- Resend integration for internal notifications.
- Proposal checklist generator for selected opportunities.

## Phase 3

- PDF/RFP parser for requirement extraction.
- Deeper scoring signals from documents and attachments.
- CRM-style follow-up tracking.

## Phase 4

- SAM.gov expansion (optional).
- Multi-user SaaS architecture.
- Stripe billing and tenant management.
